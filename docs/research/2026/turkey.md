# Turkey employee payroll, income year 2026 — clean-room research dossier

**Scope and access date.** Independently reconstructed from Turkish primary official sources, accessed **2026-10-04**. The model is a Turkish-resident employee, age 30, single, no children, in ordinary private employment under Social Insurance Law no. 5510 art. 4/1(a), receiving twelve equal monthly instalments of regular gross cash salary throughout calendar 2026. There are no other deductions, benefits, disability reliefs, employer incentives, or changes of employer.

Amounts are Turkish lira (TRY). Calculations retain fractions of a lira to make the formula auditable; an operational payroll reports in kuruş and may differ by a few kuruş after monthly rounding.

## 1. Primary official sources

1. **Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı (GİB), Income Tax Law no. 193, “Madde 103 — Esas Tarife”, updated for 2026 by General Communiqué no. 332.** The official article page gives the 2026 wage tariff verbatim: 15% to TRY 190,000; 20% to TRY 400,000; the wage-only 27% band to TRY 1,500,000; 35% to TRY 5,300,000; and 40% above TRY 5,300,000, including the cumulative tax amounts at each breakpoint. <https://gib.gov.tr/mevzuat/kanun/433/madde/6937>. **GİB, “332 Seri Nolu Gelir Vergisi Genel Tebliği”**, *Resmî Gazete* no. 33124 (5th repeated issue), **31 December 2025**, is the underlying 2026 indexation instrument. <https://gib.gov.tr/mevzuat/kanun/433/teblig/11853>.

2. **GİB, “Asgari Ücretin Hesabı”, 2026 table, effective 1 January–31 December 2026.** It states gross minimum wage **TRY 33,030.00**, employee SGK at **14% = TRY 4,624.20**, employee unemployment at **1% = TRY 330.30**, income tax zero, stamp tax zero, and net minimum wage TRY 28,075.50. It also records that Law no. 7349 made minimum wage free of income and stamp tax from 1 January 2022. <https://cdn.gib.gov.tr/api/gibportal-file/file/getFileResources?objectKey=arsiv%2Fyardim-kaynaklar%2Fyararli-bilgiler%2FAsgariUcrettenYapilanKesintiler.pdf>.

3. **GİB, “2026 Ücret Geliri Vergi Rehberi”, §§4.3 and 4.3.2, published 2026.** Section 4.3 explains Income Tax Law art. 23/18: the exempt wage is the current gross minimum wage less employee SGK and unemployment contributions; the employee’s real tax brackets are nevertheless selected including the exempt amount; and the benefit cannot exceed the tax calculated on that month’s minimum wage. Section 4.3.2 gives the calculation order for above-minimum wages. <https://intvrg.gib.gov.tr/hazirbeyan/assets/pdf/DUYURU_UNIVERSAL_2026_2026_Ucret_Geliri.pdf>.

4. **GİB, “319 Seri No.lu Gelir Vergisi Genel Tebliği”, art. 4, published in *Resmî Gazete* no. 31732 on 27 January 2022.** The enduring implementation rule says wage, premium, bonus, board fee, overtime and similar payments in the same period enter the cumulative wage base, while the exemption is applied only once for the period. The same language is reproduced in GİB’s official ruling on premiums and bonuses. <https://gib.gov.tr/mevzuat/kanun/433/ozelge/21388>.

5. **GİB, official ruling quoting Income Tax Law art. 61, “Ücretin tarifi”.** It states that salary remains salary regardless of being labelled an allowance, advance, **premium or bonus (ikramiye)**, expense payment or another name. <https://gib.gov.tr/mevzuat/kanun/433/ozelge/21377>.

6. **Sosyal Güvenlik Kurumu (SGK), “Prime Esas Kazanç Miktarları”, dated 14 January 2026, section 1.1(a), private-sector employees.** For 1 January–31 December 2026 the official daily lower base is **TRY 1,101.00**, monthly lower base **TRY 33,030.00**, daily upper base **TRY 9,909.00**, and monthly upper base **TRY 297,270.00**. The page explains that the upper base is nine times the lower base under Law no. 5510 art. 82. It also states, in its “ücret dışı ödemeler” section, that bonuses and premiums first enter the payment month; any excess over that month’s ceiling may enter the following **two months**, subject to those months’ ceilings. <https://www.sgk.gov.tr/Content/Post/2e0c9e1a-2cfe-4456-af10-49d3de0c58ba/Prime-Esas-Kazanc-Miktarlari-2026-01-14-10-35-39>.

7. **SGK, “İşveren Prim Oranları”, dated 13 January 2026, table “Hizmet Akdine Tabi Olanlar … 4/1(a)”.** The ordinary rates are: old-age/disability/death 9% employee and 12% employer; general health 5% and 7.5%; short-term insurance 0% and 2.25%; unemployment 1% and 2%. Totals are **15% employee** and **23.75% employer**. <https://eskisehir.sgk.gov.tr/Content/Post/c7812ea8-5087-413f-aeb5-d3c1d153e11a/Isveren-Prim-Oranlari-2026-01-13-04-52-38>.

8. **Ministry of Labour and Social Security, “Çalışma Hayatı İstatistikleri E-Bülteni — Şubat 2026”, issue 38.** Its 2026 table confirms gross minimum wage TRY 33,030, net TRY 28,075.50, effective 1 January–31 December, and **unincentivised employer cost TRY 40,874.63**, comprising the 21.75% SGK employer share plus 2% unemployment. <https://csgb.gov.tr/yayinlar/calisma-hayati-istatistikleri-e-bulteni/subat-2026/ucret-ve-sendikal-istatistikler.html>.

9. **GİB, Stamp Tax Law official material.** The official table/rate material gives **7.59 per thousand (0.759%)** for papers concerning salary payments. ## 2. Universal 2026 payroll rules

### Contribution base and rates

For a complete month with gross cash wage `g`:

```text
monthly SGK base c = min(max(g, 33,030), 297,270)
employee SGK       = 14.00% × c
employee unemployment = 1.00% × c
employer SGK       = 21.75% × c
employer unemployment = 2.00% × c
```

The 21.75% employer SGK consists of 12% old-age/disability/death, 7.5% health and 2.25% short-term insurance. The tables deliberately use the full **23.75%** employer total. The widely used two-, four- or five-point discounts, minimum-wage support, investment support and targeted hiring incentives are conditional public subsidies, not universal rates, and are excluded by the task assumption.

### Cumulative wage income tax

Employee SGK and unemployment contributions reduce the income-tax base. Define cumulative taxable wage through month `m` as:

```text
C_m = sum from month 1 through m of (gross wage − employee SGK − employee unemployment)
```

Define the 2026 wage tariff `F(x)`:

```text
0 ≤ x ≤ 190,000:       F(x) = 0.15x
190,000 < x ≤ 400,000: F(x) = 28,500 + 0.20(x − 190,000)
400,000 < x ≤ 1,500,000:
                         F(x) = 70,500 + 0.27(x − 400,000)
1,500,000 < x ≤ 5,300,000:
                         F(x) = 367,500 + 0.35(x − 1,500,000)
x > 5,300,000:         F(x) = 1,697,500 + 0.40(x − 5,300,000)
```

Gross income-tax withholding for month `m` is `F(C_m) − F(C_(m−1))`. The taxable base and brackets reset at the start of the next calendar year.

### Minimum-wage income-tax exemption

The monthly minimum-wage taxable amount is:

```text
33,030 × (1 − 14% − 1%) = 28,075.50
```

Let `M_m = m × 28,075.50`. The monthly exemption is `F(M_m) − F(M_(m−1))`, capped at that employee’s calculated tax for the month. For a full-year employee it is:

| 2026 months | Monthly exemption |
|---|---:|
| January–June | TRY 4,211.325 each |
| July | TRY 4,537.750 |
| August–December | TRY 5,615.100 each |
| **Full-year total** | **TRY 57,881.20** |

This schedule arises because cumulative minimum-wage tax base passes TRY 190,000 during July. All requested salaries generate enough tax to use the exemption in full. Therefore, for these equal-pay full-year examples, total payroll income tax is exactly `F(C_12) − 57,881.20`.

### Stamp tax and net pay

For these cash wages, monthly stamp tax after the minimum-wage exemption is:

```text
stamp tax = 0.759% × max(0, g − 33,030)
```

Then:

```text
employee cash net = gross − employee contributions − income tax − stamp tax
employer cost      = gross + employer SGK + employer unemployment
```

## 4. Bonuses, annual filing, incentives, and timing boundaries

- **Bonus/“13th salary”.** Income Tax Law art. 61 does not create a separate tax category: premium and bonus labels do not change wage character. GİB requires them to join the cumulative wage base, with the minimum-wage exemption applied only once in that pay period. SGK first adds a non-wage bonus to the payment month; an amount stranded above that month’s contribution ceiling is carried into at most the next two months, subject to each later ceiling. Therefore the equal-month examples cannot be reused for a lump-sum 13th payment without month-by-month recomputation.

- **No incentive assumed.** The raw 23.75% employer rate is used. No four-point general private-sector discount, manufacturing five-point treatment, two-point treatment, minimum-wage support, regional/investment incentive, young/female/unemployed hiring support, or other scheme is netted from cost. Eligibility and funding conditions make those employer-specific, not employee-payroll universals.

- **Annual return at high income.** Under art. 86, a single employer’s withheld wages exceeding the 2026 fourth-band amount of TRY 5,300,000 require annual declaration. On either a gross-income or net-tax-base comparison, the TRY 10m and TRY 30m examples clearly exceed it, while the TRY 5m example does not. With only this one fully withheld wage and no further deduction, recomputing under the same tariff and crediting withholding should not change the tax totals shown; the filing obligation remains operationally important.

- **Payment pattern matters.** Income tax is cumulative by calendar month; SGK has a monthly cap; the minimum-wage exemptions are monthly; and bonus SGK overflow has a two-month carry rule. Only the expressly modelled twelve equal full-month payments permit the compact annual arithmetic above.

## 5. Evidence assessment and unresolved facts

**Strongest evidence.** GİB’s live art. 103 page and Communiqué no. 332 state the exact 2026 wage brackets. SGK’s January 2026 base and rate pages state both the precise lower/upper bases and the unincentivised employee/employer split. The Ministry’s February 2026 bulletin independently reconciles the full employer rate to the published minimum-wage employer cost.

**Weakest evidence / variable items.** The 0.759% proportional stamp rate is carried by the statutory table and official GİB rulings; the 2026 Communiqué mainly republishes indexed fixed amounts, so the rate is less conspicuous than the income-tax table. Bonus timing, a mid-year hire, multiple employers, and eligibility for SGK incentives are fact-dependent and cannot be inferred from annual gross. No such facts were guessed. The calculations also omit payroll-specific contractual benefits and any voluntary pension deductions because none were assumed.
