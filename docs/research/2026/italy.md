# Italy employee payroll and income tax, 2026 — independent research dossier

**All sources accessed:** 2026-10-04  
**Currency:** euro (EUR)  
**Result type:** final annual economic liability and employer cost, not a reconstruction of each payslip withholding.

## 1. Fixed facts and why they are necessary

The taxpayer is age 40, resident for all of 2026 in the **Comune di Milano, Regione Lombardia**, single, with no children or other dependants, disability, other income, deductible expense, itemised tax credit, housing relief, pension-fund contribution, benefit in kind, bonus, overtime, or severance payment. Milan is selected because both the region and municipality publish their own official surtax rules; a location-free “Italian net salary” would not be reproducible.

The employment is fixed as a full-time, permanent, non-managerial office employee (`impiegato`) of a private wholesale/retail employer in the INPS tertiary sector, CSC **7.01.xx / 7.02.xx**, averaging **no more than five employees**. The employee performs only office activity. The employer has no hiring relief or other contribution exemption. This narrow classification is needed because Italian non-pension contributions vary by sector, headcount, worker status, and risk.

The employee first entered compulsory pension insurance after 31 December 1995. The statutory 2026 pension contribution maximum therefore applies. The employee elects to leave accruing TFR with this small employer, rather than transfer it to supplementary pension provision. The employer is below the 2026 INPS Treasury Fund threshold.

Annual gross cash compensation `G` includes contractual thirteenth and fourteenth instalments but excludes TFR. For timing, `G` is assumed to be earned uniformly and paid as 14 equal contractual instalments. The 13th/14th are not extra amounts on top of `G`.

## 3. Universal rules versus model choices

| Item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| 2026 national IRPEF scale and employment credit | National statutory rule |
| Lombardy and Milan surtaxes | Location-specific; not a national result |
| FPLD maximum | Applies only because post-1995 first insurance is assumed; an older entrant generally has no such cap |
| Employee/employer minor contributions | Sector, headcount, status, and relief dependent; small tertiary employer and `impiegato` selected |
| INAIL | Risk and employer-experience dependent; 0722 Terziario mean 4‰ selected |
| TFR | Statutory deferred remuneration; retained by selected small employer and not current employee cash |
| 13th/14th | Contractual pay timing, not a universal extra percentage; already included in `G` |
| Hiring incentives, welfare, bonuses, overtime, collective welfare funds | Assumed absent; not inferable from annual gross alone |

## 4. Contribution formulas

Let:

```text
G = annual gross regular cash remuneration
P = min(G, 122,295)                 pension-capped base
H = max(P - 56,224, 0)              additional-1% base
```

### Employee

```text
FPLD IVS                    = 9.19% × P
FIS                         = (0.50% / 3) × G = 0.1666667% × G
additional pension charge  = 1.00% × H
employee contributions SS  = sum of the three
```

The 1% charge is within the pension maximum. Minor FIS continues above that maximum.

### Employer INPS

The selected ordinary contribution construction is:

| Component | Employer rate/base |
|---|---:|
| FPLD IVS | 23.81% of `P` (33.00% less employee 9.19%) |
| NASpI ordinary | 1.31% of `G` |
| Interprofessional training | 0.30% of `G` |
| TFR Guarantee Fund | 0.20% of `G` |
| CUAF | 0.68% of `G` |
| Sickness | 2.44% of `G` |
| Maternity | 0.24% of `G` |
| FIS employer two-thirds share | 0.333333% of `G` |
| **Uncapped minor total** | **5.503333% of `G`** |

Thus employer INPS is `23.81% × P + 5.503333% × G`. No fixed universal employer rate should be reused outside this exact classification.

### INAIL and TFR

The worked model uses the tariff-item 0722 Terziario **mean** INAIL rate: `0.40% × G`. An actual employer's notified rate can differ; this is the most important variable assumption in employer cost.

For the first year, before any revaluation of earlier balances:

```text
TFR service accrual = G / 13.5 - 0.50% × P
```

The 0.50% reduction is limited to the base on which that pension contribution is actually due. TFR is paid on termination (or transferred under the relevant rules), taxed separately then, and is not subtracted from current take-home pay. Future revaluation of prior TFR is 1.5% plus 75% of the applicable ISTAT consumer-price increase; no future inflation rate is guessed here.

## 5. Tax calculation order

```text
R = G - employee mandatory contributions
```

With no other income or deductible costs, `R` is employment income, comprehensive income, and the base for national IRPEF and both surtaxes.

### National gross IRPEF

| Taxable slice | 2026 rate |
|---:|---:|
| 0–28,000 | 23% |
| 28,000–50,000 | 33% |
| Over 50,000 | 43% |

### Ordinary employment tax credit

For 365 work days:

```text
1,955                                              if R <= 15,000
1,910 + 1,190 × (28,000 - R) / 13,000             if 15,000 < R <= 28,000
1,910 × (50,000 - R) / 22,000                     if 28,000 < R <= 50,000
0                                                  if R > 50,000
plus 65 if 25,000 < R <= 35,000
```

The permanent employee “tax-wedge” measure is separate:

```text
tax-free cash sum, if R <= 20,000:
  7.1% × employment income when income <= 8,500
  5.3% × employment income when 8,500 < income <= 15,000
  4.8% × employment income when 15,000 < income <= 20,000

additional IRPEF credit, if 20,000 < R <= 40,000:
  1,000                                             through R = 32,000
  1,000 × (40,000 - R) / 8,000                     from 32,000 to 40,000
```

Only the 4.8% tax-free sum applies in the five examples. The older EUR 1,200 `trattamento integrativo` does not apply at EUR 20,000 gross because, for this no-other-credit taxpayer, the statutory comparison of qualifying credits with gross tax is not satisfied.

### Lombardy and Milan

Lombardy surtax is progressive:

| Slice of `R` | Rate |
|---:|---:|
| 0–15,000 | 1.23% |
| 15,000–28,000 | 1.58% |
| 28,000–50,000 | 1.72% |
| Over 50,000 | 1.73% |

Milan surtax is zero if `R <= 23,000`; otherwise it is `0.8% × R` on the entire base.

## 7. Thirteenth/fourteenth salary and surtax cash timing

- A thirteenth or fourteenth pay period changes payslip timing, not these annual results, when `G` is fixed and all instalments are ordinary contribution- and tax-bearing salary. Collective agreement and classification decide whether the fourteenth is owed; it is not a universal statutory surcharge.
- The additional 1% is operated during the year using the monthly EUR 4,685 reference and reconciled against the EUR 56,224 annual threshold. Fourteen instalments can make individual months uneven, but full-year reconciliation produces the modeled annual amount under the assumptions.
- The annual tables assign 100% of the 2026 Lombardy and Milan economic liability to 2026. Actual payslip cash timing differs. Regional surtax is normally determined at year-end and withheld in the following year. Municipal tax uses a 30% current-year advance, withheld from March in up to nine instalments, and a later balance in up to eleven instalments.
- Therefore a literal “cash withheld during calendar 2026” cannot be derived from 2026 salary alone: it also needs 2025 taxable income, 2025 year-end reconciliation, hire date, and payroll schedule. In a continuing steady-state job the timing washes through, but it must not be confused with the 2026 liability calculated here.

## 8. Evidence assessment and unresolved variables

**Strongest evidence.** The 2026 national brackets are directly in the 2026-effective TUIR and Law 199/2025. INPS Circular 6/2026 directly states the EUR 122,295 maximum, EUR 56,224 additional-1% threshold, and effective date. The Lombardy and Milan authorities directly publish their respective rates and Milan exemption.

**Weakest evidence.** INPS does not publish a convenient annual public matrix for every CSC/headcount/qualification combination. The chosen employer rate is reconstructed component-by-component from INPS's official wholesale/retail table, the current INPS 33% IVS statement, and the later/current FIS sources. It is reproducible for the facts stated, but any different CSC, headcount, contractual status, relief, or INPS auxiliary code changes it.

The 4‰ INAIL figure is only the official Terziario mean for item 0722. The actual rate communicated to a real employer reflects the 2026 oscillation system and claims/prevention record. Replace `0.40% × G` with the employer's notified rate for a production result.

The calculations exclude collective-agreement welfare/health funds, bilateral bodies, meal benefits, supplementary pension contributions, hiring incentives, productivity bonuses, overtime, and payroll administration. Their presence and allocation require the actual CCNL, grade, employer registrations, and employee elections. No value is guessed for them.

For an employee with pension insurance before 1996, do not apply the EUR 122,295 IVS maximum; the EUR 200,000 and EUR 600,000 results would be materially different. Uneven remuneration, multiple employers, partial-year work, or a mid-year move also requires pay-period and residence-specific recalculation.
